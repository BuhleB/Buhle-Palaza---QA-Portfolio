MY ROLE

Project and contribution

During my systems developer internship, I contributed to Forgot Password ROI, a team application that displays an advertisement during the password-recovery journey, captures product interest and continues to account recovery. My work covered web functionality, form validation, testing and debugging.

TESTING FOCUS

Validation across the user journey

The workflow highlights several areas for test design: whether selecting Yes reveals the purchase-date field, whether invalid email input is rejected, whether submitted interest is stored correctly, and whether recovery continues through the reset request and password-confirmation steps.
