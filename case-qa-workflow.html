TEST DESIGN

Make expected behaviour explicit

  1. Define the check: identify the requirement or observable outcome, test data and preconditions.
  2. Exercise alternatives: include invalid credentials, missing required fields and other relevant negative paths.
  3. Record the result: compare actual behaviour with the expected outcome and retain the report or screenshot.
  4. Investigate: distinguish product behaviour from locator issues, demo limitations and missing requirements.
  5. Verify changes: retest the affected behaviour and check related workflows when the application is under my control.

The historical artifacts document seven OrangeHRM manual cases and 70 SauceDemo manual cases. Those scope counts are separate from automated executions.
